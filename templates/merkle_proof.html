ℹ️ How Merkle Proofs Guarantee Privacy and Integrity

In this voting system, each block constructs a binary Merkle Tree out of its committed transactions (Concept 21). When you verify an inclusion proof:

  • You provide only your transaction receipt ID.
  • The validator produces $O(\log N)$ sibling hashes connecting your leaf to the block's published Merkle root.
  • If the calculated root matches the block header, your ballot is guaranteed to be immutably recorded in the ledger without requiring the disclosure of anyone's candidate choice or personal identity!
